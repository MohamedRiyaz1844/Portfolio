**Draw Dream** is an open-source **screenplay writing application** crafted with **React**, **TypeScript**, and **SWC**, designed to elevate your storytelling experience. Whether you're scripting your next indie film or drafting scenes for a blockbuster, Draw Dream provides the tools to bring your vision to life.

---

## ✨ Features

- ✍️ **Built-in Screenplay Formatter**  
  Write in industry-standard format without the hassle.

- ⚙️ **Automatic Content Generator**  
  Kickstart your script with structured scenes and suggestions.

- 🤖 **AI-Assisted Sentence Rewriter**  
  Enhance your dialogue and narration with intelligent rewrites.

- 🖼️ **AI-Powered Storyboard Creator**  
  Generate vivid storyboard images directly from your scenes.

- 🌙 **Dark Mode Ready**  
  A sleek, eye-friendly interface for late-night writing sessions.

- 📱 **Fully Responsive**  
  Optimized for all screen sizes—from mobile to desktop.
