**Draw Dream** is a source-available 🎬 **screenplay writing app** built with **React**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **SWC**, crafted to elevate your storytelling experience. Whether you're scripting an indie masterpiece or a blockbuster idea, Draw Dream gives you the tools to bring your vision to life — beautifully and intelligently.

---

## ✨ Features

- ✍️ **Built-in Screenplay Formatter**  
  Write in industry-standard format without the hassle.

- ⚙️ **Automatic Content Generator**  
  Kickstart your script with structured scenes and smart suggestions.

- 🤖 **AI-Assisted Sentence Rewriter**  
  Improve your narration and dialogue with intelligent rewording.

- 🖼️ **AI-Powered Storyboard Creator**  
  Turn your scenes into vivid visuals with AI-generated images.

- 🌙 **Dark Mode Ready**  
  Write in style and comfort — day or night.

- 📱 **Fully Responsive**  
  Use it anywhere: phone, tablet, or desktop.
